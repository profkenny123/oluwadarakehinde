About Akan

Homegrown. Experienced. Digitally ready. The candidate Nigeria needs.

ICAN • NIM

Chartered Accountant

Hon. Oluwadara Kehinde, popularly known as “Akan”, represents a new wave of competent, experienced, and technocratic young leadership in Nigerian politics.

Native to Owode Ketu and born and raised in Ayetoro, his connection to the people of Yewa North and Imeko-Afon is organic and lifelong.

He spent 10 years in the Ogun State Civil Service under the Office of the Accountant General.

A Chartered Accountant (ICAN), member of NIM, and certified in Cybersecurity GRC, with international exposure from Maryland, USA.

Transparent, tech-driven, and youth-led governance for the National Assembly.
