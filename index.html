About

A son of the soil. Ready to serve.

Oluwadara Kehinde (Akan) is the Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC) candidate for the Yewa North/Imeko-Afon Federal Constituency in the 2027 general elections.

He is committed to accountable representation, youth opportunity, quality education, agriculture, and people-centred development across every ward of the constituency.

His approach is transparent, tech-driven and rooted in the communities he seeks to represent — from Ayetoro to Imeko, from Ibese to Ilara and beyond.
