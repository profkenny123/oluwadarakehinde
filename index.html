About Akan

Homegrown. Experienced. Digitally ready. The candidate Nigeria needs.

ICAN • NIM

Chartered Accountant

Hon. Oluwadara Kehinde, popularly known as “Akan”, represents a new wave of competent, experienced, and technocratic young leadership in Nigerian politics.

Native to Owode Ketu and born and raised in Ayetoro, his connection to the people of Yewa North and Imeko-Afon is organic and lifelong. He understands firsthand the daily realities of local farmers, market women, artisans, and youths.

He spent 10 years in the Ogun State Civil Service under the Office of the Accountant General (Ministry of Transportation and Ministry of Community Development & Cooperatives), giving him an insider’s understanding of public finance, budgeting, and constituency project delivery.

A Chartered Accountant (ICAN), member of the Nigerian Institute of Management (NIM), and certified in Cybersecurity Governance, Risk & Compliance (GRC), he also brings international exposure from professional work in Maryland, USA.

Transparent, tech-driven, and youth-led governance for the National Assembly.
