Today · Sokoto IDP

Obi’s 12th IDP visit — shelter, toilets, clinic, school, then home

Tuesday 29 Sept: after the town hall, Peter Obi walked a Sokoto IDP camp. Makeshift rooms holding up to 10 people, no adequate toilets, no PHC, no basic school. He asked government to fix the camp and the insecurity that emptied the villages. This is the human-security line to share today.

Town hall · No fake promises

Sokoto town hall: no vote-buying, stop corruption, then subsidy

Monday: Obi told NDC members at Sokoto Guest Inn that agents will not be given money to buy votes. Nigeria’s problem is corruption. Education after unity and security. Rail to connect state capitals. Subsidy only after leakages are cut.

Palace · Sultan

Obi & Kwankwaso at the Sultan’s palace — unity and security

Channels / palace statement: the ticket paid a courtesy visit to Sultan Muhammad Sa’ad Abubakar III. The Sultan has known Obi since UNN in 1980. Message: leadership will be accounted for; treat diversity as strength; campaign without insults.

Local ground

Sawonjo, Sunwa, Imeko & Ebute — pair Sokoto with our faces

Gallery still holds 15 Sept Sawonjo and Sunwa ward videos plus Imeko team and Ebute clips, Ilara and Ayetoro stills. Today’s supporter task asks volunteers to share the IDP visit and no-fake-promises line with those local files.
