Still viral · Sokoto

Sultan visit, town hall, then Ramin Kura IDP camp

28–29 September: Obi and Kwankwaso paid a courtesy call on the Sultan of Sokoto, then a town hall — no fake promises, education after unity and security, subsidy only after corruption is removed. Obi closed with the Ramin Kura camp: makeshift rooms, weak toilets, no PHC, no basic school. “As long as even one Nigerian lives in an IDP camp, we are all internally displaced.”
